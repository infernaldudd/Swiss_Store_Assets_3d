# Swiss Store Assets 3D

Seven illuminated Swiss storefront signs, one double-sided A-frame pavement chalkboard, and one fictional Swiss chips bag based on supplied storefront photographs: **LOOP**, **LOOP restaurant**, **AMIGOS**, the **AMIGOS double-A projecting sign**, a **single A**, a **triple A**, and a **Swiss-style AUSGANG / EXIT sign**.

The assets are intended for use in Unreal Engine 5.7 and other glTF-capable engines. All dimensions use **metres**. The original nine exported `.glb` assets are in `assets/`. The 88 extended fictional snack assets are in `assets/chips/`. The three new signs have optional CPU-rendered previews generated by `RENDER_PREVIEWS=1 blender -b --python tools/build_additional_signs.py`. The signs have genuine 3D letters, visible depth, varied PBR materials, emissive faces, metal supports, and separate meshes for practical editing.

## Import

Import `assets/*.glb` using Unreal's **glTF Importer** plugin or drag them into a glTF-compatible engine. Front faces point toward the viewer; the original Blender geometry uses X horizontal, Z vertical, and negative Y outward. Place the back of the signs against the shop facade. Emissive materials make the letters luminous; for visible screen-space light spill activate Bloom and/or add local lighting in your level. Emission alone is not guaranteed to light nearby geometry.

## Swiss A-frame sidewalk chalkboard

Photo references provided by the user show an A-frame pavement blackboard with stained timber, weathered corners, exposed hinges, and hand-drawn café chalk menus. The new prop matches that construction but features **original fictional** artwork: *LOOP LUNCH* on one side and *AMIGOS OPEN* on the reverse. Built in real-world metres; approximately 0.68 m wide × 1.11 m tall, with a realistic folding footprint. Both chalk decals are separate UV-mapped mesh surfaces and can be replaced independently by assigning another image texture in Blender or Unreal. Exported GLB embeds both artwork textures and the oak-grain texture. PNG sources are committed under `textures/aframe/`.

To regenerate:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_chalkboard_textures.py
blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_aframe_chalkboard.py
RENDER_PREVIEWS=1 blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_aframe_chalkboard.py
```

Two optional rendered previews under `previews/`: `Swiss_AFrame_Cafe_Chalkboard_Front_LUNCH.png` and `Swiss_AFrame_Cafe_Chalkboard_Back_OPEN.png`. The Blender scene preview lights and pavement are deliberately omitted from the GLB export. Not intended for use as an official street sign.

## Schwefel Paprika 90g | fictional Swiss snack packaging

A fully 3D, realistic shelf prop inspired by the orange Swiss paprika potato chips bags, **not the actual brand packaging**: broad printed front label reading **SCHWEFEL Paprika**, illustrated chips, peppers, landscape; original fictional product details on the reverse. **This is a fictional game prop, not a real food product or nutritional label.** Real-world sized 0.18 m wide × 0.275 m tall × ~0.077 m inflated depth. Genuine curved foil geometry with micro-folds, heat-compressed upper/lower seams, a back fin-seal, 2K image maps, normal and roughness detail. Both sides are independently UV mapped; source textures remain editable. Source Blender file stores packed artwork. Import `assets/Schwefel_Paprika_90g.glb` to Unreal Engine or edit `source/Schwefel_Paprika_90g.blend`.

To regenerate the prop from scratch (system Python + Blender, with Pillow and NumPy):

```bash
/usr/bin/python3 tools/generate_schwefel_textures.py
blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_schwefel_bag.py
RENDER_PREVIEWS=1 blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_schwefel_bag.py
```

PNG source maps: `textures/schwefel_paprika/`. Studio previews: `previews/Schwefel_Paprika_90g_Front.png` and `previews/Schwefel_Paprika_90g_Back.png`. Avoid applying the glTF importer at 100× scale: Blender exported metres.

## Extended Swiss chips collection (88 new models)

The collection adds **88 individually exported GLB packages** across eight *fictional* Swiss snack brand families, using current Coop/Migros category listings and publicly documented legacy flavours as creative references. Existing Schwefel Paprika 90 g remains separate and unchanged; the collection totals **97 individual GLB assets** if all builds succeed. These are not claims that every flavour or size was historically sold: exact dates, former SKUs and discontinued ranges require retailer archives. Do not treat the resulting game assets as actual retailer stock or official commercial packages.

- `catalog/chip_collection.json` — the 88-item structured specification (brand, flavour, nominal package weight, shape, source category and fictional status).
- `assets/chips/<variant_id>.glb` — 88 individual 3D meshes, with UV-mapped original front and back pack images embedded in the GLB; bag shapes have physical pillow volume, side gussets, crimped foil seals and a rear fin seam. Staxo is a cylindrical stacked-chip tube with cap and rims.
- `textures/chips/` — original illustrated front/back artwork, plus shared micro-wrinkle normal and foil roughness maps.
- `previews/chips/<BRAND>_variants.jpg` — contact sheets covering all variants by brand.
- `previews/chips/Preview_9_models_3D.png` — Cycles studio overview of nine representative physically modelled products.

## Instant noodle cups — 8 sealed packs + opened hero

All packaging is original and fictional, for Swiss grocery store shelves in Helvetia and GundeliTheftAuto7 (not real Coop/Migros/Nissin products). Product variants:

- LOOP Chicken, Beef, Curry (65 g)
- AMIGOS Spicy Chicken, Vegetable, Hot Curry (70 g)
- SparFix Budget Original (65 g)
- ALP RAMEN Fire & Chili Premium (85 g)
- An additional **opened ALP RAMEN hero** with peeled foil, three-dimensional curly noodles, broth, chopped spring onion, carrot, corn, chili flakes and tofu.

Each closed model is independently exported under `assets/noodle_cups/<name>_Closed.glb`. The opened hero is `assets/noodle_cups/ALP_Ramen_Extra_Hot_OPEN_HERO.glb`; its packed Blender source is `source/ALP_Ramen_Extra_Hot_OPEN_HERO.blend`. These game objects are hollow tapered cups with a lightly irregular paper sleeve, printed foil lid, bent pull tab, moulded rolled rim, PP-style inner surface, and basal foot. PBR artwork and material maps are in `textures/noodle_cups/` (embedded in the GLBs). The dimensions are modelled in metres, and the cup circumference is UV-mapped so the printed product label faces world -Y. The 3D hero is not edible or certified packaging.

An artwork contact sheet and four CPU renders are available under `previews/noodle_cups/`. Geometry QA checks GLB 2.0 headers, independently exported meshes, printed UVs, PBR images, and nonempty file sizes. The render files are useful for review but should not be treated as a guarantee of scan-level photorealism.

To reproduce from Python / Blender (system Python with Pillow, NumPy and Lato fonts):

```bash
/usr/bin/python3 tools/generate_noodle_cup_textures.py
/usr/bin/python3 tools/make_noodle_cups_contact_sheet.py
blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_noodle_cups.py
RENDER_PREVIEWS=1 blender -b -t 2 --python tools/build_noodle_cups.py
```

In Unreal Engine 5.7 import the GLBs with the glTF importer. For the hero, adjust the broth / noodle mats separately as needed. Shared foil and paper artwork allows future flavour expansions with the generator.


## Canned foods: 18 closed tins + 2 opened hero variants

The Swiss pantry shelf collection includes 18 original fictional foods in a variety of correctly differentiated metal-tin profiles: standard 400–430g tins, squat 160g tuna tins, and a tall 565g pineapple tin. Brands are **LOOP**, **AMIGOS**, **SparFix**, and **ALP PANTRY**. Flavours and pantry categories include ravioli in tomato sauce, pelati and chopped tomatoes, sweetcorn, garden peas, kidney beans, coconut milk, chickpeas, mushrooms, tomato soup, peaches, pineapple rings, baked beans, mixed vegetables, tuna in brine and olive oil, lentil stew, and apricots.

- \`catalog/canned_food.json\`: source catalogue listing all 18 products, content types, approximate dimensions and opening types.
- \`assets/canned_food/<id>_CLOSED.glb\`: 18 separate cans with pressed steel structural walls, rolled double seams, embossed recessed tops, ring pulls on easy-open models, fine metal normal/roughness, and individual original UV-wrapped 2K matte paper labels.
- \`assets/canned_food/loop_ravioli_in_tomato_sauce_OPEN_HERO.glb\`: opened ravioli tin with 3D pasta and sauce.
- \`assets/canned_food/loop_sweetcorn_OPEN_HERO.glb\`: opened corn tin with a separate, batched mesh of individual kernels.
- \`source/canned_food/\`: Blender source files for both opened hero models.
- \`textures/canned_food/\`: original illustrated 2048×1024 label artwork and shared steel/paper PBR maps.
- \`previews/canned_food/\`: four brand assortment label sheets and five optional CPU-renders for geometric/appearance review.

The printed branding and illustration were newly generated for fictional game products. The packaging is not copied from real supermarkets and the contents, labelling, and numerical weights are **game props only**. Do not use as real nutritional information.
